Writing tips
  • Write to you, the person reading. The voice talks to one stranger at a time.
  • Each sentence is typed as its own line. Short lines you break yourself stay as they are.
  • A blank line between paragraphs becomes a long silence. ... becomes a hesitation.
  • Your own pause: [p:1500] waits 1.5 seconds. A line starting with > is set in italics.
  • {clock} and {weekday} become the reader's time and day.
